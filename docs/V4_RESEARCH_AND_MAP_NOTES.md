# V4 source & mapping notes

## Public scientific map
Scharf, A., Mattern, F., Al-Wardi, M., Frijia, G., Moraetis, D., Pracejus, B., Bauer, W., & Callegari, I. (2021). Appendices to The Geology and Tectonics of the Jabal Akhdar and Saih Hatat Domes, Oman Mountains. Geological Society, London, Memoirs, 54. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1144/M54.7. Licensing: CC BY 4.0. Figure A.1 transformed to WebP, credited and linked in the UI. Original figure itself includes underlying map credits. The publication itself notes mismatches between 1:100,000 and 1:250,000 mapping of some Hawasina units; therefore any correlation should preserve map scale and local context.

## Concession map
External publicly downloadable PDF: https://opaloman.om/theme_opal/static/src/img/files/Concession_Map_21_Sep_2021.pdf . Date: September 21, 2021. Ministry-related, hosted via OPAL. Shown as an embedded *historical* 2D map, not as a GeoJSON layer. The 2026 tender data remains separate (https://mem.gov.om/public/news/admjny0t9ejljoa685vo23tl). GIS polygons cannot be recovered with survey accuracy from a block area/number and should not be represented as precise shapes.

## Added formation catalogue
Additional units and their relative age bands are derived from the published Fig. A.1 map legend; the `source`/`sources` fields carry the cited source identifier `scharf2021map`. Lithology details not in that legend are blank/marked unspecified. This project never infers sensitive subsurface well data from a public map.

## Source of uploaded course notes
A 9-page personal set of field/lecture notes was reviewed as orientation, but its claims, sketches, thicknesses and mapped locations were not republished as independent authoritative information. Relevant surface-unit labels were cross-checked against Scharf et al. (2021).
